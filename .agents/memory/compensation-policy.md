---
name: Compensation policy
description: Wallet compensation versus payment reversal and responsibility attribution.
---

Treat responsibility attribution separately from financial liability. Full refunds, selected-item refunds, and courtesy credits are admin decisions paid into the customer wallet; they do not automatically debit driver or restaurant earnings.

**Why:** The user requested compensation channels according to reviewed responsibility, not automatic payroll or settlement penalties. Attribution alone is not evidence sufficient to impose a deduction.

**How to apply:** Require an explicit decision and explanation rather than automatically mapping a responsible party to a payout. Cap compensation at the order total unless a new policy is authorized. Item amounts use stored order-line values with integer-cent proportional rounding; preview and server calculation must agree. Courtesy and item credits are not provider reversals and must not claim that the original card transaction was reversed.
